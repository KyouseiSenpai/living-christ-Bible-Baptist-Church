Ministries

A core part of our teaching is turning God-given talents into God-glorifying gifts by His grace—helping each member be available and willing to do His work through the ministries God opens inside and outside our church.

Ministry of the Word

The proclamation and sharing of God’s Word wherever people are found—from the pulpit to print, audio, video, and other media—so that sinners may hear the Gospel of Christ clearly.

Ministry of serving

The use of practical gifts—skills, time, hospitality, music, building care, and help for families—in every sphere where the Lord opens a door to serve His people and advance the work of the church.

What we believe

We hold to the fundamentals of the faith: the verbal inspiration of Scripture, the deity of Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, substitutionary death, bodily resurrection, ascension, and imminent return; salvation by grace through faith; the local church; and the Great Commission.
